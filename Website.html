FAQ

Questions before you start?

Is this suitable if I'm a complete beginner?

Yes. The coaching process starts from your current level and builds from there. You don't need to already be an expert in the gym.

Do I need to train five days a week?

No. The exact approach can be adapted around your schedule and experience. The goal is to create something you can consistently execute.

Will I get a meal plan?

Coaching uses personalised nutrition guidance rather than a one-size-fits-all rigid menu. Your approach is based on your goals, preferences and lifestyle.

How do check-ins work?

You'll provide progress information so we can review adherence, performance and changes over time and adjust the plan where needed.

How long does the coaching run?

Coaching is offered monthly. The APEX 12-week method gives you the structure for the first phase, while ongoing coaching keeps the process moving.
